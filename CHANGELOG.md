# Changelog

Each release of the `chessdrive` Plugin bumps `version` in `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` and adds a line here, newest first. People who added the marketplace get it from Check for updates, or `claude plugin marketplace update` and `claude plugin update` in Claude Code. A zip doesn't update, and uploading over it changes nothing: people remove the Plugin, download it again and upload it.

- **0.28.1** (2026-11-17): Through the Connector, where no code runs, the Skills no longer lose a chess model's words. A note or a reply loses a sentence only when it links a move that wasn't legal, or says the opposite of the engine; a move written as plain text, or "a mistake" for a blunder, stays, and a note left empty is asked for once more. `chessdrive-position` says what goes under "The plan" when nothing is left, and passes the engine's Lines the same way each time. Game review and a Study now agree: evaluations in words on a review's page, the same turning points, one message to the chess models, "On the board" as the top card, and a hand-over that says what the page's Study key allows. A Study built through the Connector copies each move once from the tool's answer, takes its API address from its key, and a Mistake drill keeps the engine's Line hidden until you've tried.
- **0.28.0** (2026-11-17): `chessdrive-prep` downloads only what it reads. It takes an opponent's newest 1,000 games at blitz or slower, about a minute, and still counts their newest 200 with your colour; before, it downloaded every game, over 6 minutes for an account of 9,000. It starts without asking which games. The chess model that writes the plan is told what the engine says about the line they score worst in, so it no longer guesses who stands better from the moves. "What this rests on" accounts for every game in the file: games with the other colour and games they didn't play are counted, and the numbers add up. `openings.py` writes what it prints to a file with `--out`, so the Study is built from one run, and it reads a Lichess game with no clock as correspondence.
- **0.27.0** (2026-11-17): A script builds a Game review's Study, so your agent no longer writes the page by hand. `study.py` makes it from the engine's answer and the notes the review saved: the Board with every verdict, the top card with the verdict and note of the move on view, the turning points and the graph of White's chance to win. Every note on the page is the chess model's, word for word, and a move no model explained says so. Annotate's page is built the same way. The page is a start: your agent can add to it or change it for you, such as a section on one opening move, and when it does it keeps every note as written and checks the page again with `check.py`.
- **0.26.9** (2026-11-16): A Study's "Engine's line" works in Claude's chat app. An artifact there lets the page load the engine's script but not its `.wasm` file, so the button waited 30 seconds and said the engine can't run. The page now notices and loads the same Stockfish 18 as one script instead, slower, with each search stopped after 5 seconds. A page opened as a file loads the engine as before.
- **0.26.8** (2026-11-16): `chessdrive-patterns` says 30 games take about 4 minutes on every Plan, as a Free run on production measured, and no longer says 8 on Free.
- **0.26.7** (2026-11-16): A back rank mate in `chessdrive-game-search` is one where pawns and the mating piece alone shut the king in. The board after a mate says whether other pieces help, and the checks turn those down, such as a queen mate on c8 where a rook and a knight box the king in too.
- **0.26.6** (2026-11-16): `chessdrive-game-search` asks Knight about each hit as a coach would. Knight gets the board after the move in words, from a new `board.py` that reads it off the game, and the idea's checks from `references/ideas.md`, such as a back rank mate's rook or queen away from the king and the king shut in by its own pawns, and goes through them before it says yes. `judge.py` asks it, asks once more about each yes and keeps a hit only on two, drops a note that names a piece the board doesn't have, and names every hit Knight didn't answer, with why. Over 480 Lichess games, back rank mates went from 47 wrong yeses in 65 to none.
- **0.26.5** (2026-11-15): A Study gets its "Ask about this position" card, its key, its campaign in the footer and safe text whichever Skill sends your agent to build it, Game review's and Prep's included. The Board faces you in an opponent prep. In a coding agent the page is saved in your folder even when the app offers a scratch folder, and its working files stay out of it. The hand-over names the moves that cost most, gives no reason the chess model didn't, and keeps to three lines. A worksheet with "no answers on the sheet" keeps its answer key on its own page.
- **0.26.4** (2026-11-13): `chessdrive-game-review` writes every Line it gives with all its moves, so none comes out illegal, links only to what a tool gave it whole, keeps a chess model's note when it drops one wrong sentence from it, and asks about every marked move, inaccuracies too. The models are told "an inaccuracy", not "a inaccuracy".
- **0.26.3** (2026-11-12): The Skills know ChatGPT. There they make every call with the ChessDrive Connector, take a game from a link with `get_game` and show positions with `show_board`, and Patterns, Game search and Vectors say they need Codex, Claude Code, Cursor or Claude's chat app. This README says what the Skills run and send, and lists Prep.
- **0.26.2** (2026-11-10): With the CLI at 0.7.0, which sends as many Embed calls at once as your Plan allows, embedding is almost twice as quick on Hobby and over three times on Pro: 1,000 games take about 5 minutes on Hobby and 3 on Pro. `chessdrive-game-search` and `chessdrive-vectors` quote both CLIs' times.
- **0.26.1** (2026-11-10): Embedding your games is about 30 times quicker: 200 games take about 2 minutes on any Plan, against 53 on Free before, as Embed now runs on a GPU. `chessdrive-game-search` and `chessdrive-vectors` quote the new times.
- **0.26.0** (2026-11-06): ChessDrive's models make every chess call, and the first answer comes sooner. Game review and Annotate explain every move the engine marked: Queen each mistake and blunder, up to 12 a game, and Knight each inaccuracy in a line, so no "Better was" line is filled in for you, and a move the models couldn't explain says so. A Game review Study shows the move's verdict and explanation in its top card. Knight, not your agent, checks that a search hit or a group of mistakes shows the idea. The Skills ask the chess models up to 6 at a time with a shared script, `ask.py`, and build the page while they answer. Your agent never opens a Study in a browser to check it: `check.py` checks the page, and a page that hits an error says so on it, in a line to paste back. The Patterns Study gets the "Ask about this position" card.
- **0.25.1** (2026-11-06): `chessdrive-patterns` says 30 games take about 5 minutes on every Plan: Free now checks six games at a time, as Hobby does, so a run on Free no longer takes 8.
- **0.25.0** (2026-11-05): `chessdrive-patterns` groups your mistakes by what they allowed as well as by the board. The chess model writes one line on each mistake from the engine's lines, eight a call, Embed takes the lines and the positions, and the groups come from both, so a name fits its examples more often: on two players' games, two names in three held, against one in three before. Each example's explanation says how it shows its group's name where the engine's line does. The Skill quotes about 45 cents and 5 minutes, or 8 on Free, as two runs measured; the chess model's lines are about 3 cents of that, and under a minute.
- **0.23.1** (2026-11-05): `chessdrive-game-review` says who stood where in the engine's own words. Its script prints "about equal", "a little better", "clearly better" or "winning" for the position before and after each move, from the evaluation's band, and the answer copies them, so a move that threw away a win is no longer called a draw thrown away. A video or article gets its Study too, a heading's mark is its verdict's, no inaccuracy joins the list while a mistake or blunder of yours is there, a reply the chess model cut short by its own thinking is asked for once more, and a reply's claim of a piece won or a named pattern stays only when the engine's Line shows it.
- **0.23.0** (2026-11-05): The game fetcher downloads only the games you want. `fetch_games.py` takes `--speed`, one or more of bullet, blitz, rapid, classical and correspondence, and `--since` and `--until` dates: Lichess sends only those games, and chess.com reads only the months in range and keeps their games of that time control. `--how-long` counts the games of that scope. When a player has over 500 games and you didn't say which, your agent asks which time controls and how far back before it downloads, so "my rapid games" no longer waits for every bullet game.
- **0.22.0** (2026-11-04): One position costs nothing. A Study's "Engine's line" runs Stockfish in your browser, free, with no key, and its questions still use the page's Study key. Where code runs, `chessdrive engine` analyses one position below depth 20 on the Stockfish installed on your machine, free, and says how to install it if there's none. ChessDrive's Engine, now $4.50 an hour of Engine time, does whole games, lists of positions, several games at once and searches at depth 20 or more. `chessdrive-patterns` quotes about 35 cents. The free local engine needs the next CLI release; an older CLI runs every position on ChessDrive's Engine.
- **0.21.0** (2026-11-04): The Skills call ChessDrive through the `chessdrive` CLI wherever your agent runs code, Claude's chat app included, and use the Connector only in a chat that runs none, or if you'd rather not install the CLI. Game review gets a Lichess or chess.com game from its link on your machine. `chessdrive-patterns` marks your mistakes with `chessdrive engine --games` and a short example your agent adapts, so "count my inaccuracies too" counts them. No Skill names a Plan's limits any more: the CLI reads them from ChessDrive. Needs CLI 0.6.0.
- **0.20.0** (2026-11-04): `chessdrive-patterns` keeps a group's name only when at least three of the five examples checked show it, and says how many groups it left out. Each group it keeps shows three examples from different games, each with a line from the chess model on what the move allowed, from the engine's lines, and a script builds the Study in the frame, so the run is quicker: about 4 minutes, or 5 on Free.
- **0.19.0** (2026-11-04): A Study answers about the position you're on. Its "Ask about this position" card shows the engine's evaluation and best Line, and answers one question about the position with that Line, from Queen, or Knight on Free past Queen's limit. It runs only when you ask. A Study built by a coding agent comes with its own Study key, which calls only the engine and the chess models, up to $1 a day, for 7 days, and is listed on the Dashboard under Study keys. A downloaded copy carries no key: it shows a quiet "Add your key" line, and a key added there stays in that browser. In Claude's chat app the page can't reach ChessDrive, so a Study there works as before. Needs CLI 0.6.0 for `chessdrive study-key`, or the Connector's `study_key` tool.
- **0.18.0** (2026-11-04): Every Study looks like ChessDrive and has the same layout: a header that says what it is and how to use it, the Board, and a panel beside it, or under it on a phone or in a narrow artifact. The Board and its buttons stay in view on any screen. In a coding agent the Study opens by itself and its path comes as a link, and Download saves a copy that opens with no internet, or Save as PDF. Agents no longer test the page in a browser.

- **0.16.0** (2026-11-03): Annotate's talking points read as a video script: each is a bold line to say on camera, then two or three sentences on what's going on, why it matters and what to point at, from the chess model's replies and the engine's lines, then the Line to show.
